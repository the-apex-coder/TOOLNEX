Compress Images Online for Free

TOOLNEX Image Compressor helps you reduce image file sizes directly in your browser. Choose an image, adjust the compression quality and select your preferred output format. Download your compressed image when processing is complete.

Supported Image Formats

This tool supports JPG, PNG and WebP image formats. You can choose WebP, JPG or PNG as your output format using the available settings.

How to Compress an Image

  1. Select or drag and drop an image into the upload area.
  2. Adjust the compression quality using the slider.
  3. Choose your preferred output format.
  4. Click the Compress Image button.
  5. Download your compressed image.
