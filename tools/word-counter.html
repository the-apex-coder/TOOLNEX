Free Online Word and Character Counter

TOOLNEX Word Counter helps you analyze text instantly. Check the total number of words, characters, characters without spaces, sentences and paragraphs. You can also estimate how long your text may take to read.

Who Can Use This Word Counter?

This free word counting tool is useful for students, writers, bloggers, developers, journalists and content creators. Use it to check essay word limits, prepare articles, review assignments and analyze written content.

How to Count Words Online

  1. Type your text or paste it into the text editor.
  2. View the word and character counts instantly.
  3. Check sentences, paragraphs and estimated reading time.
  4. Use Copy Text to copy your content or Clear Text to start again.

Is My Text Uploaded to a Server?

Your text is processed directly in your browser by this tool. It does not need to be uploaded to a TOOLNEX server for counting.
