Create QR Codes Online for Free

TOOLNEX QR Code Generator lets you create custom QR codes directly in your browser. Generate codes for website links, plain text and email addresses. Customize the foreground and background colors to create a QR code that matches your preferred style.

QR Code Types You Can Generate

Create QR codes for website URLs that people can scan to open a web page. You can also generate codes containing plain text or email addresses.

How to Generate a QR Code

  1. Select the type of QR code you want to create.
  2. Enter your website URL, text or email address.
  3. Choose your preferred QR and background colors.
  4. Click the Generate QR Code button.
  5. Download your generated QR code as a PNG image.

Customize and Download QR Codes

Choose colors that suit your design and download the generated QR code as a PNG image. For reliable scanning, use good contrast between the QR code and its background.
