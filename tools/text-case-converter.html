Free Online Text Case Converter

TOOLNEX Text Case Converter helps you quickly change the capitalization and formatting of text. Whether you are editing documents, writing articles, preparing assignments or formatting content, use this free tool directly in your browser.

Available Text Conversion Options

  • UPPERCASE: Changes all letters to capital letters.
  • lowercase: Converts all letters to small letters.
  • Title Case: Capitalizes the first letter of each word.
  • Sentence case: Capitalizes the beginning of sentences.
  • Capitalized Case: Capitalizes the first letter of each word.
  • Remove Extra Spaces: Cleans unnecessary spaces from text.

How to Convert Text

  1. Type or paste your text into the input area.
  2. Select your preferred text conversion option.
  3. View the converted text in the output area.
  4. Copy the result or download it as a text file.

Private and Browser-Based Processing

Your text is processed locally in your browser using JavaScript. The converter does not need to upload your text to a TOOLNEX server.
